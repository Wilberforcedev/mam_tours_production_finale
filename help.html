Help and travel FAQs

Useful answers about what this demonstration site can and cannot do today.

Can I book a trip here?

No. This is a front-end demonstration and does not create reservations or collect payment.

Are the prices and routes current?

No. All routes, duration and prices shown are sample content. Live travel availability and supplier terms are not connected.

Can I save a journey?

The homepage’s save control stores selections in your browser only. It does not create an account or sync across devices.

How do I contact MAM Tours?

Use the contact page to see the current demonstration contact options. Placeholder details must be verified before launch.

Can I sign up for travel updates?

The homepage newsletter form provides a local confirmation only. It does not send or store your email address.
